## 1.0 — 2026-11-12 13:10 PST

No public skill covered a deep read of unfamiliar codebases ending in a prioritized plan a non-technical owner can act on, so this one was built from the method used by hand on two client repos. It measures each job first and scales from one small repo to several large ones. It runs six fixed phases (size up, map, investigate, verify, synthesize, deliver), labels every claim confirmed, likely or unverified, has a separate agent try to disprove critical and high findings, and delivers four layers: decision page, how it works, findings register, evidence appendix. Ideas borrowed from tech-debt-audit, codebase-review, codebase-to-course and graphify.
On Ben's request.
